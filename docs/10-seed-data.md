## Invariants (`pnpm verify:seed` must assert all of these)
1. No stock item is ever negative at any point in the history.
2. Finished stock = produced − sold ± adjustments, per product. Purchase stock = received − used ± adjustments.
3. Every invoice total equals the sum of its lines, recomputed with `calcInvoice`. Paid amount equals the sum of allocated payments. Statuses are consistent with due dates.
4. Customer ledger closing balance equals the dues report. Cash and bank ledgers equal receipts minus payments, expenses and salaries.
5. Fund received minus utilised equals the fund tracker balance. Loan outstanding equals loans minus repayments.
6. Last month's settlement recomputed with `calcSettlement` matches the stored figures to the paisa, and payslips sum to the worker total.
7. Document numbers are unique and gapless per financial year (except cancellations).
8. Every business record has an audit entry. Every worker with a login has attendance sessions.
9. The state document size is under 1.5 MB.
